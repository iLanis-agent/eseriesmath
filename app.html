E-Series Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page picks standard resistor values from the number you type: the nearest E24 and E96 single values, the closest E24 series pair, and the closest E24 parallel pair, each with the signed error against your target. The target is yours; the tables are the published E24 and E96 base values across seven decades. It does not know the tolerance, the power rating, the temperature drift or the availability of any actual part, and it gives no verdict that a picked value suits your circuit. Every number here is your own entry.
